# Vocabulary study protocol decisions — 2026-09-12

The study owner approved the following design directions for the pilot and SAP:

- large-document pre-reading audits are predetermined probability samples with
  positive inclusion probabilities; selected-card outcomes are estimated from
  selected items inside that sample, never added post hoc;
- participants may supply multiple context-free meanings/translations; blinded
  raters see restricted target context and preserve `criterion-ambiguous`;
- literal human theta-interval coverage is omitted absent an independently
  defensible latent reference; synthetic theta recovery remains unchanged;
- confirmatory collection is blocked by a developmental randomized pretest-
  interference experiment with a frozen meaningful margin;
- calibration uses a 7.5% expected eligible-opportunity rate, not a forced
  per-session quota, plus a protocol-frozen anchor bank; ordinary anchors must
  occur in the current document;
- calibration production activation, confirmation execution, and release-
  holdout access remain unauthorized.

These decisions update the research protocol direction but do not choose the
SAP estimator, variance method, numeric sample size, interference margin,
non-inferiority margin, or confirmatory acceptance thresholds. Those fields
remain unapproved and block confirmatory collection.
