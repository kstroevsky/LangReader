# Vocabulary pretest-interference developmental protocol v2

Status: **response-modality aligned; real collection blocked**.

This v2 addendum supersedes v1 for future collection while retaining v1 by
checksum for every unchanged assignment, rater, dependence, missingness,
privacy, and access contract.

The criterion task remains lemma+POS followed by one or more context-free
meanings/translations. The product probe is now the actual default first-stage
interaction: `I know it`, `Not sure`, `I don’t know`, plus `Not a word/name` as
a separate exclusion action. Typed meaning remains optional exactly as in the
product.

The primary outcome is the indicator that the first-stage action is `I know
it`; it is not adjudicated meaning correctness. The complete categorical
distribution and exclusion rate are retained, while optional typed meanings may
be scored secondarily when present.

Assignment remains 1:1 within participant/document and frequency/POS stratum:

- criterion task, then product first-stage probe; or
- product first-stage probe, then criterion task.

To keep the control free of criterion exposure before its product response, a
known claim is captured but its normal definition reveal is delayed until the
other task is sealed. This is an instrumentation deviation after the primary
first-stage response and remains a transportability limitation; no claim about
the full adaptive session is made.

The primary ITT contrast is:

`Pr(known claim | criterion first) - Pr(known claim | product first)`.

Equivalence still requires the approved interval to lie wholly inside
`[-interferenceMargin, +interferenceMargin]`. All v1 analysis, margin, support,
consent, and governance fields remain null. V2 additionally requires a frozen
product UI contract revision, reveal-delay implementation, and known-claim
coding version. Real collection and every holdout/production authorization
remain false pending independent approval.
