# Vocabulary response-protocol alignment checkpoint — 2026-09-14

## Resolved audit findings

The two substantive identification findings and two terminology findings are
implemented in additive v2 protocols. V1 artifacts and their rehearsals remain
immutable historical readiness evidence.

### Pretest response modality

The criterion remains a context-free meaning/translation task. The product
probe now uses the actual default first-stage UI: `I know it`, `Not sure`,
`I don’t know`, and separate `Not a word/name` exclusion, with typed meaning
optional. Its primary outcome is the first-stage known-claim indicator, not
rater-scored meaning correctness.

To retain a no-criterion-before-product control, the developmental instrument
captures a product-first known claim but delays its normal reveal until the
criterion task is sealed. That post-response timing deviation is explicit and
prevents a full adaptive-session transport claim.

### Repeat production evidence event

`E1` and `E2` now each include the complete production path:

- first-stage categorical action and optional typed meaning;
- pending-known state with no evidence recorded;
- definition reveal;
- `My meaning was correct` or `No / only partly`; and
- exact mapping to `verifiedKnown`, `typedVerifiedKnown`,
  `verifiedUnknownOrPartial`, `unsure`, `reportedUnknown`, or `excluded`.

The repeat protocol states that the pre-E2 action is not an unaided baseline
because the E1 reveal may teach, cue, or reinforce. Baseline `K` depends on an
approved criterion-acquisition path after the pretest-interference decision.

The primary denominator now says **distinct eligible participant-item records**;
participant/document/item dependence remains mandatory.

### Tail-risk terminology

The frozen target-shortfall formula is unchanged. Its clearer display alias is
`conditionalTargetShortfall`. A separate
`conditionalThresholdExcessSeverity = E[c_severe - C_realized | C_realized < c_severe]`
is defined but has no approved release role or tolerance.
